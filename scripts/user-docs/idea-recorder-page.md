# Idea Recorder

Record your demos, riffs, or quick ideas here. An idea is one or more takes and, if you like, a board of notes; hitting Record starts the next take.

## How to use the Idea Recorder

Press Record and play or sing; press Stop and the take is saved to the idea and listed under Recordings, numbered in order. Switch sources in and out under the screen: the microphone, a line in, the computer's audio, the piano and the drums, in any mix. With two or more in, the take is multitrack: the mix as usual plus a file for each source, and a Tracks panel appears under the recorder to hear them on their own. Name the idea in the field above the screen, keep notes beside it, and send a take to a song as a demo or as stems when it is ready.

### Quick Tips

- Each source's meter runs as soon as it is in, so check your level before you press Record.
- The small button beside a source opens its settings: device, channels, gain and, for an instrument on an interface, one channel on both sides.
- Open the drums or the piano from the toolbar to play along; their sound goes into the take while their button is lit.
- Press New Idea to start a fresh idea; the notes clear and the takes start again at one.
- The recorder's settings (the gear) choose lossless or compressed takes and whether silence is trimmed from the ends.

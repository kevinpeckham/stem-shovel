# Mixes

A **mix** is a bounce of the song, usually from the mixing engineer or producer, sent to the band to listen to and comment on. After the feedback a new mix follows, and the loop repeats, so mixes are numbered: v1, v2, v3.

## Where they are

The player on the song page switches between **Stems**, **Demos** and **Mixes**. The Mixes view opens on the newest mix, with every earlier one listed under it; click one to switch.

## Listening

The chosen mix has a play button, the time, a volume slider and its waveform. Click the waveform to jump there. The **Comments** row under it marks every comment left at a spot in the mix; click a marker to read it.

## Commenting on a mix

Comments on a mix stay with the mix: they do not appear in the song's Comments tab or on the stems' Comments row, and the song's own comments do not appear here.

- **At a spot**: hold ⌘ (Mac) or Ctrl (Windows), or right-click, on the mix's waveform or its Comments row, and choose **Comment here**. The position is filled in.
- **About the mix in general**: press **Comment** beside the transport.

Anyone who can comment on the song can comment on its mixes. Your own comments can be edited and deleted; owners and admins can delete anyone's.

## Notes from the mix

Under the waveform, **Notes from the mix** is where the engineer says what changed and what to listen for. Editors write them with **Add notes** or **Edit notes**; a little markdown works (lists, bold).

## Uploading, naming, removing

Editors upload with **Upload Mix** at the foot of the view, or from the **Uploads** menu. Any audio a DAW exports works (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, M4A and more, up to the stem size limit); the mix is named after its file and can be renamed from its menu. The menu also downloads the file (or its MP3), shares the mix by email, and removes it, comments and all. A song keeps up to 24 mixes.

## Sharing a mix

**Share by email** in a mix's menu sends a link that opens the song page on that mix. For a private song the link is a viewing link made for the recipient, as the song's own share is.

## Hearing about mixes

A new mix reaches everyone on the project in their inbox, and a comment on a mix tells everyone which mix it is about; both links open the song on the mix. Email follows the demos setting (**Settings → Notifications**).

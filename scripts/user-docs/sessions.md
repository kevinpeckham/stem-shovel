# Sessions

**Sessions** is the band's shelf of tapes: recordings of practices and shows, uploaded by any member of the account and there for every member to hear and download. Find it in the account menu at the top right, under Projects.

## Uploading a session

Choose **Practice** or **Show**, set the day (today to begin with) and, if you like, the place, then pick one or more audio files: a phone's voice memo, a recorder's WAV, a board mix from the venue, in any of the formats a demo accepts (MP3, M4A, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, CAF and the rest). Each file becomes a session of its own; a long evening can be one big file, up to 2 GB. The recordings count toward the account's storage like everything else.

## Browsing

Sessions are listed by day, newest first, with their kind, title, place, who uploaded them and how big they are. **All**, **Practice** and **Show** at the top narrow the list. Press play on any session to listen right there; **Download** saves the file as it was uploaded.

## Editing

Any member can change a session's title, kind, day, place and notes with **Edit**, and remove one with **Delete** (the file goes with it). The title starts as the filename minus its extension, so a quick rename after the upload is the usual move: "Tuesday run-through", "Set two, the Elbow Room".

Sessions belong to the account, not to a project or a song: a practice covers the whole set. Keep the notes for what was played, what worked and what to fix next time.

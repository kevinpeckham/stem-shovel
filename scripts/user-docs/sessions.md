# Sessions

**Sessions** is the band's shelf of tapes: every practice and every show, with its recordings, uploaded by any member of the account and there for every member to hear and download. Find it in the account menu at the top right, under Projects.

## Sessions and recordings

A **session** is one practice or one show. It has a kind (Practice or Show), the day it starts, a place and notes, and a name of its own if you give it one ("Battle of the Bands"); without a name it is called after its place and day ("The Elbow Room · Oct 18, 2026"). A session's **recordings** are the audio files of that evening, each with its own title, day and notes, in the order they were made. A show that runs past midnight is still one session: its recordings may be of the day it starts or the day after, never further.

## Uploading

Pick one or more audio files: a phone's voice memo, a recorder's WAV, a board mix from the venue, in any of the formats a demo accepts, up to 2 GB a file. Each file is dated by its own clock (the recorder's, not the upload's) and goes into the session of that day, or the day before's if that is where the evening started, or into a new session of that day named after the place you typed. To put files somewhere particular, choose the session first. An MP3 of each recording renders in the background for playing and for the session's download; the row says "MP3 rendering…" until it is there. Everyone else in the account gets a notification.

## Listening

The player at the top plays a session's recordings in order: press **Play all** on a session, or the play button on any recording, and use the arrows for the previous and next one. Space plays and pauses, Home goes back to the start. **Download MP3s** on a session saves every rendered MP3 of it in one zip, numbered in order; each recording's menu has its own MP3 and the original file.

## Sorting recordings into sessions

Two sessions on one day, an acoustic set in the afternoon and the show at night, say: choose **New session on this day…** in a session's menu, name it, then open each recording's **Edit** and pick the session from the list, which offers the sessions of the recording's day and the day before, or **New session…** to make one right there. A session's **Edit** changes its name, kind, day, place and notes; **Delete session** removes it with every recording in it.

Sessions belong to the account, not to a project or a song: a practice covers the whole set. Keep the notes for what was played, what worked and what to fix next time.

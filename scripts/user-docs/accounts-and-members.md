# Accounts and members

An account is the workspace a group shares: its projects, songs and members. **Account settings** in the account menu is where owners and admins manage it.

## Roles

- **Owner**: created the account; can do everything.
- **Admin**: everything an owner can, including inviting people and managing settings.
- **Member**: uploads, edits songs and documents, comments.
- **Viewer**: the same for now; kept for finer control later.

Anyone can view any song by its link regardless of role.

## Inviting by email

Enter an address and a role and send. The person gets an email with a link; if they do not have an account yet, the link lets them create one with that address and joins them straight away. Pending invitations are listed and can be revoked.

## Invite codes

A code is for handing out in person or pasting into a message. Generate one with a role, an optional note (who it is for), a use limit and an expiry; copy the code or the sign-up link, and revoke it when it should stop working. Anyone who signs up with it joins the account.

## Usage

The settings page shows how many projects, songs and stems the account holds and how much storage they use.

## Managing members

In **Account settings**, owners can change anyone's role, including making someone else an owner to hand the account over, and remove members. Admins can set members and viewers and remove them. The last owner cannot leave or be demoted.

## Belonging to several accounts

You can be an owner of one account and a member of others. The account menu at the top right shows your role in each and switches between them; **Your accounts** lists them all, and you can leave any account there. Pages outside an account, such as the docs, treat the account you last opened as yours.

## Starting another account

Once you belong to any account you can start another of your own: **New account** in the account menu, or on the Your accounts page. You become its owner and invite people from its settings.

## Your own workspace

Someone who signs up with a new-account code gets a workspace of their own. Joining through an invitation or an account's code puts you straight into that account, without a separate workspace.

# Chat

Every song has a chat: a running conversation for the people on the project. The speech-bubble button at the top of the song page, beside Share, opens it as a panel of its own, so you can talk and read the chart or lyrics at the same time. Comments are for notes about a spot in the song; the chat is for talking.

## Who sees it

Anyone who can comment on the song: the account's members, and the people added to the project, viewers included. Visitors with a share link, and anyone looking at a public song without signing in, do not see the button.

## Writing

Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** to send; **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. Messages are plain text, up to 2000 characters.

- A web address becomes a link.
- A position written as time, like **1:23** or **0:45.5**, becomes a button that jumps the player there.

## Mentions

Type **@** and start a name: a list of the people on the project appears; pick one and their name goes into the message. The person is told you mentioned them, in their inbox and, if they have email for comments and chat turned on, by email, even when the rest of the conversation is folded into one notification for them. A mentioned name is highlighted in the message; your own, when someone mentions you, more so.

## Reactions

Hover a message (or tap it on a phone) and the smiley opens a row of six: 👍 ❤️ 😂 🔥 🎸 👀. One tap adds yours, a second tap takes it back, and the message shows each emoji with a count; hover a count to see who. Reactions are quieter than a reply: nobody is notified of one.

## On the project page

Each song's tile counts its messages, and a badge beside the tile shows how many you have not read; click it and the song opens with its chat up. The **Chat** button in the project's header opens one feed of every song's recent messages in time order, each with its song's name; the name opens that song's chat, which is where you reply, since a conversation lives on its song.

## Reading

Messages run oldest to newest, with a line where the day changes. A **New** line marks where you left off, and the chat button shows a dot while there are messages you have not seen. The chat refreshes itself every few seconds while the panel is open.

## Editing and deleting

Hover a message of your own for **Edit** and **Delete**. An edited message says so. Account owners and admins can delete anyone's message.

## Notifications

A new message reaches everyone on the project in their inbox, folded into one item per song. It goes to email with the same setting as comments (**Settings → Notifications**).

## Sessions

A practice or a show on the Sessions page has a chat of its own, from the **Chat** button in the session's header. It works the same way, for everyone in the account, with a dot on the button for messages you have not seen; a time in a message jumps the session player there.

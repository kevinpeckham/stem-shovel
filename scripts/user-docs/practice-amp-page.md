# Practice Amp

Plug a guitar or a bass into your computer or phone and play it through an amp, with pedals, in your headphones or speakers.

## How to use the Practice Amp

Choose Guitar or Bass, pick the head, and press the power switch: the amp opens your input (the line in of an audio interface, or the microphone jack with an instrument in it) and you hear yourself at once. Set the input with the Input menu: which device, Instrument or Line, and the trim, or press Set level and play for three seconds. Turn the knobs on the head and open the Pedals menu for the board.

### Quick Tips

- Wired headphones or the interface's own headphone jack keep the delay under 20 ms; Bluetooth adds a tenth of a second or more.
- An instrument straight into a computer's microphone jack is quiet and a little dull (the jack's impedance is wrong for a pickup); an audio interface or a guitar-to-USB adapter fixes both. Instrument mode adds 12 dB of trim for the direct case.
- Playing through the built-in speakers works with an interface or the line in; with the microphone input, the speakers feed back into the microphone.
- The tuner and the metronome open from the toolbar and share the amp's audio, so the click and your playing arrive together.
- Everything on the amp is remembered in your browser, per instrument.

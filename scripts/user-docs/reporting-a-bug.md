# Reporting a bug

Found something wrong? The **Report a bug** link in the footer (shown when you are signed in) opens a short form: a title and what happened. The page you were on and your browser are attached automatically, so there is no need to describe them.

What helps most:

- what you did, step by step, and what you expected instead
- the song or project it happened on (the page address is already included)
- whether it happens every time

Reports go straight to the people who run Stem Shovel, and replies to any follow-up email reach you directly.

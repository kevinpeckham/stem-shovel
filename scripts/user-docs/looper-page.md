# Looper

Lay a loop down a layer at a time from the microphone, the piano or the drums, then save it to the Idea Recorder as a take with each layer as a stem.

## How to use the Looper

Choose a source under Record from, press Record, and play a pass of the loop: from the next bar 1 every full pass becomes a layer until you press Finish layer. The layers play round and round while you add more, from the same source or another. Save as take puts the loop in the Idea Recorder with each layer as its own stem.

### Quick Tips

- Set the tempo, the bars and the count-in in the Timing menu before the first layer; once a layer exists they are fixed until you clear the loop.
- Tap the tempo in the Timing menu, or set it on the drum machine: the two stay in step.
- Choosing Piano or Drums as the source opens that instrument; recording the drums starts the beat on bar 1.
- Use wired headphones or the built-in speakers: Bluetooth adds a lot of delay. Calibrate in the Mic menu measures your microphone's.
- The space bar plays and stops the loop unless an instrument panel was the last thing you touched.

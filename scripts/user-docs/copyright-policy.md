- Uploading or sharing material without that right is a violation of this agreement. We may remove the material and may suspend or close the account responsible.

## Everyone else's material

If you believe material on Stem Shovel infringes a copyright you hold, email inquiries@lightningjar.com with the address of the page, a description of the work, and how we can reach you. We will look into it promptly and remove infringing material.

## Acceptable use

Do not use Stem Shovel to store or distribute anything unlawful, to harass anyone, or to interfere with the service or other users' accounts.

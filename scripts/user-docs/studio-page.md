# Studio

Record tracks over what you have, from the microphone, a line in or the computer; arrange the takes on a timeline; keep revisions; send the mix to a song as a demo or the tracks as stems.

## How to use the Studio

Add a track, arm it (the red dot), choose its input and press Record: after a bar of count-in the take lands on the track where the playhead was while the other tracks play. Stop ends the take and saves it. Press Record again for the next part, on the same track or another.

### Quick Tips

- Use wired headphones or the built-in speakers: Bluetooth adds a lot of delay. Calibrate in the Inputs panel measures your microphone's and the takes land in time.
- Drag a clip along its track or onto another; the grid snaps it to the beat, Shift drags it freely. Delete removes the selected clip, ⌘Z undoes.
- Space plays and stops, R records, Home goes to the start, L loops the region on the ruler (drag its ends).
- The song saves itself as you work; Save revision in the song's menu keeps a named snapshot you can restore from the Songs panel.
- The song's menu bounces the mix to a WAV, adds it to a song as a demo, or adds each track to a song as a stem.

# Chord Player

The piano's sounds on a circle of fifths: press a chord to hear it, hold it as long as you like, and find the chords that go together side by side.

## How to use the Chord Player

Press any wedge of the circle and the chord sounds for as long as you hold it; the outer ring is the major chords, the inner ring their relative minors, and the chords beside any chord are the ones that fit it best. Hold the 7 pad, or Shift, for a seventh. Turn the circle with the Key buttons so your song's key sits at the top. The Sound menu, the presets and the Effects menu are the piano's: a grand piano, an electric piano, an organ and three synths, with reverb, delay, chorus, fuzz and the rest.

### Quick Tips

- Play from the computer keyboard: the number row plays the majors around the circle from the top, the row below it the minors, and Shift adds the seventh.
- Notes mode turns the circle into twelve single notes, for a melody over a chord you remember.
- The Chords menu chooses the voicing: spread or rich for a fuller sound, bass for a low root under the triad.
- A strum, in the Chords menu, staggers the notes as a hand would; it suits the grand piano.
- Space is the sustain pedal and Escape stops everything, as on the piano.

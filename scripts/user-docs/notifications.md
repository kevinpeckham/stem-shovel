# Notifications

Your **inbox** collects what happens in the projects you belong to. Open it from the account menu at the top right, where a badge counts what you have not read yet.

## What lands there

- **Comments** someone leaves on a song.
- **New stems** someone uploads to a song, and **new songs** created in a project.
- **New demo recordings** added to a song.
- **Invitations accepted**: when someone joins through an invitation you sent.
- **Warnings** for an account you own or manage: storage nearly full (at 80 % and 95 %) or full, and every seat taken.

Nothing you do yourself lands in your own inbox. Several new stems on one song within a short while show as one item. Opening an item marks it read and takes you where it points; **Mark all as read** clears the badge.

## Email

**Notifications** in the account menu is where you choose what also reaches your email. Comments, stems, songs and demos are off until you switch them on. Warnings about storage and seats, and an accepted invitation, always come by email as well: they are the ones that need attention.

Choose **As they happen** to get each email at once, or **Once a day** or **Once a week** to get one summary of everything you opted into. Warnings still come at once.

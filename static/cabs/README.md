# Captured cabinets

Impulse responses of real guitar cabinets for the Practice Amp
(docs/practice-amp.md, "The cabinet"), from Jester Dyne Productions
(Bastian Karschewski, https://jester-dyne-productions.com), released
under CC0 / public domain ("All IR's are completely free to use in your
private and commercial Projects", the packs' handbooks, 2022). Trimmed
to 120 ms with a fade, 48 kHz, 16-bit mono, peak-normalized; the amp
scales each to unity over the body of the instrument as it loads.
